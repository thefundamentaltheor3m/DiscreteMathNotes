\chapter{The Local Lemma} \label{Ch5:CH}
\thispagestyle{empty}

Whenever we have wanted a random object to avoid a list of bad events, we have asked for the bad events to be rare enough that, added up, they still cannot cover everything---the union bound behind \Cref{Ch2:Thm:mk_Lower_Bound}. This chapter asks for much less: there may be far too many bad events for that, provided each one depends on only a few of the others.
