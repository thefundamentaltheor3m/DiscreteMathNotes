We now put points in the plane and ask how many pairs of them can be at distance exactly $1$. In the language of \Cref{Ch2:Def:Unit_Distance_Graph}, this asks how many edges a subgraph of the unit distance graph on $n$ vertices can have, rather than how many colours it needs. Constructions give lower bounds; the upper bound will come from drawing graphs in the plane and counting how often their edges are forced to cross.
