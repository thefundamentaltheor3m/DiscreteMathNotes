\begin{boxtheorem}
    Any Kakeya set in $\F_q^n$ has size at least $\parenth{\frac{q}{10n}}^n$.
\end{boxtheorem}
\begin{proof}
    Suppose not. Choose a nonzero polynomial $p$ of degree at most $q - 2$ that vanishes on $K$.
\end{proof}
